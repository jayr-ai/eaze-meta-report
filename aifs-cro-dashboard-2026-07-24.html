No populated attributionSource / lastAttributionSource fields (UTM, fbclid, referrer) exist at the opportunity level for any of them - re-verified fresh on this run via the same search-opportunity pull used for Section 04/05. The click-to-cash chain dead-ends at "this was a Medical-funnel ad lead" - it does not roll up onto the revenue record at the individual campaign or ad level. This is the report's core standing measurement gap and it is still open today.

08Critical Measurement Gaps (this run)

  • Booking disposition gap. 17 of 23 real demo bookings this week (74%) are past-due with no showed/no-show/cancelled status ever logged - show rate can't be trusted at a weekly cadence. Newly quantified this run; not previously sized this precisely.
  • Revenue overstatement. 2 of 7 Closed-Won opportunities ($14,994, 60% of stated pipeline revenue) have zero matching Stripe payment. GHL's own stage is not proof of cash.
  • Unreconciled real payment. One real $997 charge (Jul 15) has no GHL Closed-Won match at all - a real sale that may be sitting mistracked somewhere else in the pipeline, or entirely outside it.
  • Attribution dead-end. UTM/click-level data never reaches the opportunity or revenue record - confirmed still true this run (Section 07).
  • Campaign ID drift. The week's top-spend campaign is a brand-new "RELAUNCH" duplicate not folded into the documented canonical campaign map.
  • Test-data noise. 4 of 27 total demo bookings this week (15%) were internal test bookings; nothing currently filters these out automatically.

09ICE-Ranked Experiments

  1. Enforce same-day disposition logging on every demo call. Impact: high (unlocks real show rate & cost-per-show for the first time). Confidence: high (root cause is a manual process gap, not a data pipeline issue). Ease: medium (needs closer/EA discipline or a status-update automation).
  2. Resolve the 2 unpaid Closed-Won opportunities (Juan Valencia, Natalie Hadden). Impact: high ($14,994 of phantom revenue corrected). Confidence: high. Ease: high - collect the outstanding payment or move the stage back.
  3. Trace the unmatched $997 Stripe charge (Jul 15) to its real customer. Impact: medium. Confidence: medium. Ease: high (single Stripe customer lookup + GHL search).
  4. Fold the new "RELAUNCH" campaign ID into the canonical campaign map. Impact: medium (reporting integrity, prevents future fragmentation). Confidence: high. Ease: high.
  5. Scope a UTM-to-opportunity attribution write-back (custom field populated at lead capture, carried through to Closed-Won). Impact: high (long-term marketing ROI clarity). Confidence: medium. Ease: low - real dev work.
