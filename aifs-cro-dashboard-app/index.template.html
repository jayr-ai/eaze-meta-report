The CRO reading

This is the classic signature of scaling into a fixed audience and a small creative set: the same pool of people are being shown the same ads more often, so the auction gets more expensive (CPM up) and the ads get less novel (CTR down), which compounds into CPC up. A frequency in the 2 to 3 range is early, not yet a hard saturation signal, but it is worth a weekly check at this spend velocity, not monthly, especially if it keeps trending the same direction as the cost curves above.

Remediation
  1. Ship fresh creative into the active spend drivers before the next spend increase. Do not scale budget into stale creative this early.
  2. Expand audience in Ads Manager, Ad Set, Audience Controls for the active campaigns rather than only raising budget on the same audience, to relieve frequency pressure.
  3. Set a frequency watch: review Ads Manager, Columns, Performance and Clicks, Frequency weekly at the ad set level. If any active ad set crosses 3.0 to 3.5 with flat or falling CTR, treat it as an audience refresh trigger, not a bid trigger.
  4. Do not respond to rising CPC by raising bids or budget alone. Fix creative and audience first, then re-test spend level.
